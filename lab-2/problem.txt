Lab Report: 02
Question:
Write a Java program to demonstrate polymorphism using inheritance and method overriding. 
Create a superclass "Employee" with a method "calculateSalary()". 
Create two subclasses, "FullTimeEmployee" and "PartTimeEmployee",
and override the "calculateSalary()" method in each subclass according to their salary calculation.
Use an "Employee" reference to call the overridden methods and display the salaries of both employees.
